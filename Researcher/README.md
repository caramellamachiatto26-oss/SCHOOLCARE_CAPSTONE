## Review of Related Literature (RRL)

The literature review provided the foundation for developing **SCHOOLCARE+**, a web-based school clinic information system. Previous studies emphasized the importance of Electronic Health Records (EHR), Health Information Technology (HIT), inventory management, and Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) in improving healthcare efficiency, record accessibility, inventory tracking, and data security.

While existing systems often focus on individual functions such as medical records or inventory management, few integrate these features into a single platform designed specifically for school clinics. This research gap inspired the development of **SCHOOLCARE+**, which combines health records, inventory, appointment scheduling, reporting, and secure access management into one system.

---

## Conceptual Framework

The project follows the **Input–Process–Output (IPO) Model**.

### Input
- Manual student medical record management
- Difficulty accessing health records
- Inefficient medicine and supply inventory
- Lack of appointment scheduling
- Challenges in generating health reports
- Need for secure access to confidential information

### Process
- Requirements gathering and analysis
- System and database design
- Front-end and back-end development
- Implementation of medical records, inventory, scheduling, and reporting modules
- Authentication and Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)

### Output
A secure, web-based **SCHOOLCARE+** system that streamlines clinic operations through digital record management, inventory tracking, appointment scheduling, automated reporting, and protected access to sensitive information.

---

## Research Methodology

The development of **SCHOOLCARE+** followed a structured System Development Life Cycle (SDLC) consisting of:

1. **Requirements Analysis** – Identified school clinic challenges and defined system requirements.
2. **System Design** – Planned the system architecture, database, interface, and core modules.
3. **System Development** – Built using:
   - **Frontend:** Vite React, HTML, CSS, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS
   - **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js, REST API, MongoDB
4. **Testing and Evaluation** – Verified functionality, usability, reliability, and efficiency of modules including:
   - Student Medical Records
   - Consultation Tracking
   - Medicine Inventory
   - Appointment Scheduling
   - Automated Report Generation
   - User Authentication and RBAC

### Feasibility Analysis

The project was evaluated based on:

- **Technical Feasibility** – Availability of technologies and resources
- **Economic Feasibility** – Cost-effectiveness using free and open-source tools
- **Operational Feasibility** – Ability to improve clinic workflows
- **Legal Feasibility** – Compliance with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173)
- **Schedule Feasibility** – Completion within the planned development timeline

The evaluation confirmed that the chosen methodology is suitable for developing and implementing the **SCHOOLCARE+** web-based school clinic information system.
